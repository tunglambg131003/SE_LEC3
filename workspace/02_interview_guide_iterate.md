## Full Iterated Interview Guide

### Warm-up
1. Could you describe a typical shift for you, focusing on how you manage your daily responsibilities?
   - How do you allocate your time across different tasks during peak dining hours?
   - Where does managing delivery orders fit into your overall routine?

### Current Workflow
2. Walk me through exactly what happens in your restaurant from the moment a delivery order comes in to the moment it leaves the kitchen.
   - How do you communicate the specific details and modifications to the kitchen staff?
   - How do you balance the priority between walk-in customers and incoming delivery tickets?

3. How do you track the progress of a delivery order once the kitchen starts preparing it?
   - What steps do you take if a dish is taking longer than expected to prepare?
   - How do you handle situations where an item runs out after an order has already been accepted?

### Operational Flow & Challenges
4. Thinking about your busiest shifts, how do delivery operations impact the overall flow of the restaurant?
   - Can you walk me through a recent instance that required you to directly intervene in a delivery order?
   - Which steps in the delivery lifecycle require the most attention or effort from your team?

5. How do you currently handle situations where order volume changes significantly?
   - What strategies do you use to regulate the flow of incoming delivery requests?
   - How does a rush impact your staff's ability to maintain accuracy?

### Coordination with Riders and Customers
6. Tell me about the handoff process when a rider arrives to pick up the food.
   - What factors typically impact the handoff between your staff and the riders?
   - How do you handle situations where a rider arrives before or after the expected time?

7. Walk me through your process for handling customer feedback or complaints related to a delivery order.
   - How do you typically determine whether an issue (such as a missing item or food condition) occurred in the kitchen versus during transit?
   - What channels do you use to communicate with the customer to resolve the situation?

### Success and Priorities
8. If a new operational system were introduced, what would an optimal service look like for you and your staff?
   - What specific manual tasks would you prefer to automate or simplify?
   - What information is most critical for you to see at a glance to keep the kitchen running smoothly?

### Wrap-up
9. Is there anything else about your experience managing delivery workflows that we haven't covered today?
   - Who else on your team (e.g., a specific line cook or host) would have valuable insights on this process?

---

### Time Management Note
If the interview runs short, cut **Question 3** (tracking order progress) and **Question 5** (handling volume surges), as Questions 2 and 4 will capture the essential workflow and operational dynamics. Retain Questions 4 and 6 (rider handoffs), as they represent critical operational interfaces.
